# SoundFont redistribution

Reviewed against the upstream notices on 18 September 2026. Both supplied banks
have published terms permitting redistribution; their binaries have therefore
been retained locally. Keep their own license and copyright notices alongside
any distributed copies. The repository GPL license does not replace these terms.

- **MuseScore General:** MIT license, including its named contributors and the
  original FluidR3/FluidR3Mono notices. See
  [the complete local notice](MuseScore_General_License.md) and
  [upstream](https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/musescore/soundfont/MuseScore_General/MuseScore_General_License.md).
- **GeneralUser GS:** the author's custom license permits use in software,
  modification, music production, and hosting your own copy. The author explicitly
  discloses that the origins of some older samples cannot be fully verified; the
  permission statement is not a guarantee of every underlying sample's title.
  See [the complete local notice](GeneralUser_GS_LICENSE.txt) and
  [upstream](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS/main/documentation/LICENSE.txt).

The `.sf2` binaries remain ignored by Git, and `sources.json` records their
original URLs and SHA-256 checksums. The download script restores them locally.
The new album uses MuseScore General. No soundfont has been uploaded by this task.
